# Impeccable — project design context

## Design Context

### Users
Stellar testnet users in three roles: wallet users reading their own
confidential-token statement (the emotional core: "can I finally SEE my
private money?"), contract developers indexing their own deployments,
and hackathon judges auditing claims. Desktop-first, used at night by
crypto-native people; every promise on screen is verifiable on-chain.

### Brand Personality
Nocturnal · verifiable · serene. Umbra is the darkest part of an
eclipse shadow — the product observes quietly, remembers forever, and
proves what it says. Nothing shouts; certainty is calm.

### Aesthetic Direction
Lunar minimalism on near-black. Requested by the owner: sober black
tones + the color of the moon, with Umbreon (the Pokémon) as a loose
reference — matte black body, thin luminous gold rings. The distilled
concept is the **annular eclipse**: a black disc wearing a thin ring of
pale gold light. Rings and halos are the signature motif; moon-gold is
the only accent and it must stay rare (verification moments, the mark,
primary action). Errors take Umbreon's red-eye tone.
Anti-references: violet/neon crypto dashboards, glassmorphism, cyan-on-dark.

Palette (OKLCH, all neutrals tinted toward the gold hue ~88):
surface oklch(0.13 0.008 88) · card oklch(0.16 0.01 88) ·
line oklch(0.25 0.015 88) · ink oklch(0.93 0.015 92) ·
ink-2 oklch(0.66 0.025 90) · accent moon-gold oklch(0.82 0.12 92) ·
error oklch(0.62 0.18 25).

Type (RESOLVED 2026-08-05, owner-directed): the native San Francisco
stack (-apple-system … system-ui) for everything — one family,
hierarchy through weight and size, tight tracking (-.02em+) on large
text, sentence-case labels, no letterspaced smallcaps, no serifs, no
webfonts. The first pass used Marcellus and read as "medieval/generic"
— do not reintroduce display serifs. ui-monospace only for addresses;
tabular numerals on every amount. Glow is near-zero: the mark and
verification rings only.

Manga level (owner escalated 2026-08-05, second decision): MANGA
PROPER, still on the sober black+gold base. Display type is Dela
Gothic One (vendored latin woff2) for h1 + wordmark ONLY — the one
deliberate exception to the system-font rule; UI text stays native SF.
Result blocks are inked panels (hand-drawn CSS border trick with
asymmetric slash radii); hero carries a denser gold screentone and a
static corner speed-line burst; rough-notation v0.5.1 draws animated
ink (headline underline + circle, circle on the decrypted balance);
decryption flashes 360ms radial speed lines. All reduced-motion-aware,
all vendored. Western comic frameworks (pop-art/CMYK) remain declined.

### Design Principles
1. One accent, spent sparingly — gold appears where trust is earned
   (verification, the mark, the primary action), nowhere else.
2. Rings over rectangles — the eclipse ring is the recurring form:
   logo, verification badges, progress. Never decorative glow for its
   own sake.
3. Calm certainty — generous vertical rhythm, left-aligned, no
   card-in-card, no competing calls to action.
4. Honesty is UI — verification states (match/mismatch/unavailable) and
   coverage progress are first-class visual citizens, never footnotes.
5. The pages are the product's proof — everything shown must be
   fetchable/checkable by the viewer (no vanity metrics).
